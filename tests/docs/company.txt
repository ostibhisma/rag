# Company Overview: NexusTech Solutions
NexusTech Solutions was founded in 2020 with the vision of revolutionizing enterprise automation.
Our mission is to build intelligent systems that augment human capabilities in the workplace.

# Locations and Offices
Our global headquarters is located in Copenhagen, Denmark.
We also have a major R&D facility in Austin, Texas, USA, focused entirely on edge computing and IoT.
The European support center operates out of Berlin, Germany, handling all Tier 1 and Tier 2 customer tickets.

# Products and Services
Our flagship product, "NexusFlow-X1", is a workflow automation engine.
We also offer a specialized AI suite called "CognitiveBridge". It helps businesses integrate Large Language Models into their legacy systems.
For hardware, we produce the "EdgeNode-77A", a ruggedized computing unit for industrial environments.

# Internal Departments
The Artificial Intelligence team works heavily on machine learning, natural language processing, and retrieval augmented generation.
Our Infrastructure division is responsible for cloud deployments and Kubernetes cluster management.
The Sales and Marketing team, led by VP Sarah Jenkins, focuses on B2B enterprise software acquisitions.

# Company Policies
Employees are entitled to 25 days of paid vacation per year.
The remote work policy, updated in 2023, allows staff to work from home up to 3 days a week.
Expense reports for travel must be submitted via the internal portal "SpendTrack" within 14 days of returning.

# Financials and Growth
In Q4 2023, the company reported a record revenue of $45 million.
The company currently has exactly 142 employees worldwide.
We recently secured a Series B funding round of $20 million led by Nordic Ventures.
